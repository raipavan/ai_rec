About TalentPool365

Built by recruiters who were tired of recruiting software

TalentPool365 started in 2019 inside a 40-person agency that was drowning in resumes. The first version was an internal matching tool; it cut their screening workload by half in a month. It became a company when their clients refused to give it back.

Today the platform processes over two million interviews a year across 40 countries. The rule from day one still stands: AI does the busywork, recruiters make the decisions, and every score can be explained to a candidate, a client or a regulator.

What we optimize for

Four commitments we hire and ship by

Evidence over gut feel

Every recommendation carries its reasons. If the model can't explain it, it doesn't ship.

Fairness by design

Models are audited for bias quarterly, and results are published to customers unedited.

Humans stay in the loop

AI shortlists; people decide. No candidate is ever auto-rejected by a model.

Security is table stakes

SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and candidate data that never trains shared models. Ever.
